# Reviews & Ratings System

Build a complete, production-ready review and rating system similar to Amazon, Sephora, or Shopify.

## Product Ratings

Every product should display:

* Average rating (1–5 stars)
* Total number of reviews
* Rating distribution (5★ to 1★ bars)
* Star rating beside the product title
* Rating shown on product cards
* Rating shown in search results
* Rating shown in featured products
* Rating updates automatically whenever a review is added, edited, approved, or deleted.

---

## Customer Reviews

Users should be able to submit reviews from the product page.

Each review should include:

* Star rating (1–5)
* Review title
* Review text
* Customer name
* Optional email (hidden from public)
* Verified Purchase badge (admin controlled)
* Upload multiple images (up to 5)
* Optional short video support (future-ready)
* Review date
* Helpful/Not Helpful buttons
* Report Review option

Image uploads should:

* Preview before submission
* Compress automatically
* Open in a lightbox when clicked
* Be responsive on mobile and desktop

---

## Review Moderation

Reviews should NOT appear publicly until approved by an admin.

Review statuses:

* Pending
* Approved
* Rejected
* Spam

Admins should be able to:

* Approve
* Reject
* Edit
* Delete
* Feature reviews
* Mark as Verified Purchase
* Reply to reviews
* Pin important reviews
* Filter by product
* Filter by rating
* Search reviews
* Bulk approve
* Bulk reject
* Bulk delete

---

## Admin Review Manager

Create a dedicated "Reviews" section inside the admin dashboard.

Display:

* Total Reviews
* Pending Reviews
* Approved Reviews
* Average Rating
* Lowest Rated Products
* Highest Rated Products
* Most Reviewed Products

Each review should display:

* Product
* Customer Name
* Rating
* Review Title
* Review Content
* Uploaded Images
* Submission Date
* Status
* Admin Reply
* Verified Badge Status

---

## Admin Replies

Admins should be able to reply to customer reviews.

Replies should:

* Appear directly below the review
* Be labelled as "Official Dreamer Products Response"
* Include reply date
* Allow editing
* Allow deleting

Frontend example:

★★★★★

"Absolutely love this face serum!"

(Review text...)

📷 Customer Photos

Official Dreamer Products Response

"Thank you for your wonderful feedback! We're so happy you're enjoying the product."

## Performance

* Lazy-load review images.
* Optimise uploaded images.
* Paginate long review lists.
* Cache average ratings where appropriate.
* Keep all rating values in sync automatically.

---

## UI/UX

Design the reviews section to feel premium, clean, and trustworthy.

Use smooth animations, elegant spacing, rounded cards, responsive layouts, and a luxury aesthetic consistent with the rest of the website.

The entire review and rating system should work seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile without breaking any existing functionality.
